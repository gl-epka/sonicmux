# Code of Conduct

SonicMux follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, publication of private information, and other abusive conduct are not
acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/gl-epka/sonicmux/security/advisories/new).
Maintainers will investigate reports privately, act proportionately, and avoid
conflicts of interest. Enforcement may include correction, warning, temporary
restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.
